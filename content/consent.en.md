# Information about this study

Please read this page carefully. You can ask us questions at any time.

## What is this study about?

We are studying online shops that can change how they look to suit each person. For example, a shop could show bigger text, or labels next to its buttons.

We want to find out what people aged 40 and over prefer, and whether a shop can learn this from a few of their choices.

This study is part of the course {{courseName}} at {{university}}. It is supervised by {{supervisorName}}.

## What will I do?

The study has two sessions.

**Session 1 takes about {{minutesComparison}} minutes.** You can do it at home or with us, on a laptop or a tablet.

- First, you answer a few short questions about yourself.
- Then you watch pairs of short videos. Each video shows a grocery shop changing how it looks.
- Each time, you choose the version of the shop you would rather use. There are no right or wrong answers.
- There are two short breaks.

**Session 2 takes about {{minutesShopping}} minutes.** It takes place in person, on our laptop.

- You do four short shopping tasks in a practice grocery website. No real money is used and nothing is bought.
- The website may change how it looks while you use it.
- After each task, you answer a few short questions.
- At the end, you put the four versions of the shop in order of preference.

We will contact you to arrange Session 2.

## Are there any risks?

There are no known risks beyond normal use of a computer or tablet. You can take a break whenever you like.

There is no direct benefit to you. Your answers will help make websites easier to use for older adults.

## Do I have to take part?

No. Taking part is your choice. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason. Stopping will not affect you in any way.

## What do you record?

- the choices you make, and how long they take
- your answers to the questions
- basic information about you, such as your age, how often you use computers and your preferred language
- how you use the practice shop, such as what you click and what you put in the basket

We do **not** record your name. You get a participant code instead, for example P-012. We do not record video, sound or pictures of you.

## How is my information kept safe?

- Your answers are stored in a password-protected database and on password-protected computers.
- Only the research team ({{researcherNames}}) and the supervisor can see them.
- We report results only for groups of people, never for one person. Nobody can be identified from the results.

## Can I withdraw my data?

Yes. You will see your participant code at the end of each session. If you want your data removed, send your participant code to {{contactEmail}}. We will delete your data. You can do this until the results have been written up. You do not need to give a reason.
